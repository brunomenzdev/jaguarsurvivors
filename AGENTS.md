# Repository Instructions

Before starting a task, read [`project-management/00-governance/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md`](project-management/00-governance/PROJECT-CONSTITUTION.md), relevant entries in [`PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md`](project-management/00-governance/PRODUCT-DECISIONS.md), and the task-specific instructions.

- The human Product Owner has final authority. Follow the documented decision hierarchy and change-control process.
- Do not change product identity, the approved acidic tone, predominantly real-public-figure roster direction, Partido Missão exclusion, platform scope, core gameplay, monetization, or major architecture without explicit owner approval.
- Do not treat proposals, assumptions, audit findings, roadmaps, or subagent reports as approved requirements or implementation authorization.
- Do not expand task scope or remove existing functionality independently. Preserve historical source/assets; exclusion from the commercial product is not permission to delete repository history.
- Do not reintroduce Partido Missão content into the commercial product. Review any proposed legacy reuse separately.
- Flag legal, rights, platform, reputational, and publishing risks for real-person representation. Never treat public visibility as clearance.
- Preserve Phaser 3.70.0, Vite 5.4.21, JavaScript ES Modules, desktop-browser scope, and the existing architecture unless an approved decision changes them. Prefer incremental, evidence-based improvements; do not rewrite for convenience.
- Respect keyboard, mouse, and gamepad as initial product inputs. Keep unresolved controller models and browser targets TBD.
- Keep unresolved decisions TBD. Stop dependent work and ask when a product decision is missing or instructions conflict.
- Modify only files authorized by the task and permitted by workspace rules. Do not install/upgrade dependencies, create assets, or change gameplay unless explicitly authorized.
- Update relevant documentation after an explicitly approved decision, without overwriting unrelated documents. Keep changes traceable and report files changed and checks performed.
- Coordinate shared-file edits; avoid conflicting concurrent modifications.
